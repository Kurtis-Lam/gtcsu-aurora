Disclaimer

Terms on which this student-run website is operated under the School's supervision.

This website is operated by student members of the Students' Union Cabinet Aurora and is not the official website, agent or representative of G.T. (Ellen Yeung) College.

1. Nature of the Website and school supervision

  • The Website is a student project operated by the Students' Union Cabinet Aurora (“Aurora”) under the supervision of G.T. (Ellen Yeung) College (the “School”). The Website is not the official website of the School, and nothing on it constitutes an official statement, act or representation of the School unless expressly stated.
  • The School has the final decision on whether the Website goes live, continues to operate, is suspended or is terminated.
  • Aurora shall promptly amend, suspend, take down or delete any content or feature of the Website upon the request of the School, the Teacher-in-Charge or their designated person.
  • Technical administrative access to the Website does not constitute authority to approve content or to determine operating arrangements. Content that requires teacher or school approval is published only after approval has been given.

2. Account and system ownership

  • Administrators of the Website are added, removed or changed only with the approval of the Teacher-in-Charge and School IT.
  • Administrator accounts, secrets and access rights are not shared with any person who has not been approved.

3. Content and approval workflow

  • Content is handled under the agreed workflow: the content team drafts, the Website PIC conducts the first review, the teacher or School reviews where required, and an authorized person publishes.
  • Content that requires teacher or School approval, including sensitive matters, official announcements, financial information and content involving students' personal data, is not published without that approval.
  • Where the School considers any content inappropriate, inaccurate or unauthorized, Aurora shall immediately take it down or correct it and record the reason.
  • The content of the Website is provided for general reference only. Aurora endeavours to keep it accurate and up to date but gives no warranty as to its completeness or accuracy, and the content does not constitute an official notice of the School.

4. Data, privacy and third-party services

  • Data is processed only for purposes necessary to operate the Website, and data collection is kept to a minimum.
  • Some features of the Website involve third-party services (including Firebase, Vercel, Cloudinary, OpenRouter and Gmail). These services continue to be used only with the School's approval of the services and data flows concerned. Where the School does not approve, the relevant features shall be disabled or modified.
  • Aurora shall cooperate with the School in setting data-retention and deletion policies (including photo-download records, support records, analytics and subscription data), and shall implement and test them as approved.
  • Feedback submissions are linked to the submitter's school account. The feedback form is not anonymous and will not be described as such unless a genuinely anonymous feature has been built and tested.
  • Data collected through the Website shall not be used for any purpose beyond the operation of the Website, and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the School's approval.

5. Photo-download records and investigations

  • Watermarks and download records are tracing aids and not conclusive proof. They may support an investigation but cannot by themselves establish that a particular student edited or posted an image.
  • No student shall be publicly accused on the basis of a watermark or download record alone. Any investigation and any follow-up action are for the School to decide under School procedures.
  • Photo-download records are accessed, exported or used only when requested or approved by the Teacher-in-Charge, and access to investigation materials is restricted.

6. Security and incident response

  • No website can be guaranteed to be free from unauthorized access. Aurora does not warrant that the Website will be uninterrupted, error-free or secure at all times, and gives no such guarantee to the School or to users.
  • Security is maintained on an ongoing basis, including weekly, monthly and post-release checks, and changes are tested before release.
  • In the event of a security or privacy incident (for example a compromised account, data exposure, leaked secret, unauthorized access or service outage), Aurora shall immediately notify the Teacher-in-Charge and School IT and, as needed, contain the impact, preserve records and assist recovery.
  • The priority findings listed in the report (admin-claim governance, the public security report page, feedback privacy wording, data retention, third-party data processing, upload limits, secret hygiene and production verification) shall be addressed as soon as possible, in cooperation with School IT on verification and testing.

7. Handover and termination

  • Aurora shall keep complete technical documentation and change records. On handover or termination, source code, documentation and access rights shall be transferred, the relevant credentials rotated, and confirmation given that the School can continue to control the Website.
  • Where a member responsible for the Website leaves their role, graduates or otherwise ceases to be responsible for it, that member shall cooperate with the School on handover and shall have their administrative and access rights removed.

8. General

  • This disclaimer and the “Website Security, Photo Traceability & Operations Plan” (the “Plan”) are based on the code and configuration of the Website. The Plan is not a penetration-test or compliance certificate, and its approval is subject to School IT verifying the live deployment.
  • Aurora operates the Website honestly, transparently and responsibly, and accepts the School's supervision, review and instructions.
  • To the extent permitted by law, neither Aurora nor its members accept liability for any loss or damage arising from the use of, or reliance on, the Website. Aurora may update this disclaimer from time to time, and the latest version published on the Website prevails.
