Operator's Undertaking

Disclaimer

How this student-run website is operated under the school's supervision.

This website is operated by student members of the Students' Union Cabinet Aurora and is not the official website, agent or representative of G.T. (Ellen Yeung) College.

1. The school makes the final decision

  • I understand that the Website is a student project operated under the school's supervision, and that the school has the final decision on whether the Website goes live, continues to operate, is suspended or is terminated.
  • I agree that the school, the Teacher-in-Charge or their designated person may at any time require me to amend, suspend, take down or delete any content or feature of the Website. I will act promptly on such a request.
  • I will not treat my technical administrative access as authority to approve content or decide operating arrangements myself. Content that requires teacher or school approval will be published only after approval is given.

2. Account and system ownership

  • I will add, remove or change administrators only with the approval of the Teacher-in-Charge and School IT.
  • I will not share any administrator account, secret or access right with any unapproved person.

3. Content and approval workflow

  • I will follow the agreed workflow: the content team drafts, the Website PIC conducts the first review, the teacher or school reviews where required, and an authorized person publishes.
  • I will not publish on my own any content that requires teacher or school approval, including sensitive matters, official announcements, financial information and content involving students' personal data.
  • If the school considers any content inappropriate, inaccurate or unauthorized, I will immediately take it down or correct it and record the reason.

4. Data, privacy and third-party services

  • I will process data only for purposes necessary to operate the Website and will keep data collection to a minimum.
  • I understand that some features involve third-party services (including Firebase, Vercel, Cloudinary, OpenRouter and Gmail). I will continue using them only after the school approves the services and data flows. If the school does not approve, I will disable or modify the relevant features.
  • I will cooperate with the school in setting data-retention and deletion policies (including photo-download records, support records, analytics and subscription data), and implement and test them as approved.
  • I will make clear to users that feedback submissions are linked to their school accounts, and I will not describe the feedback form as anonymous until a genuinely anonymous feature has been built and tested.
  • I will not use data collected through the Website for any purpose beyond its operation, and I will not disclose it to any third party without the school's approval.

5. Photo-download records and investigations

  • I understand that the watermark and download records are tracing aids, not conclusive proof. They can support an investigation but cannot by themselves prove that a particular student edited or posted an image.
  • I will not publicly accuse any student on the basis of a watermark or download record alone. Any investigation and any follow-up action are for the school to decide under school procedures.
  • I will access, export or use photo-download records only when requested or approved by the Teacher-in-Charge, and I will restrict access to investigation materials.

6. Security and incident response

  • I understand that no website can be guaranteed to be unhackable, and I will not give the school or students any such guarantee.
  • I will maintain security on an ongoing basis, including weekly, monthly and post-release checks, and test changes before release.
  • If a security or privacy incident occurs (for example a compromised account, data exposure, leaked secret, unauthorized access or service outage), I will immediately notify the Teacher-in-Charge and School IT, and as needed contain the impact, preserve records and assist recovery.
  • I will address the priority findings listed in the report as soon as possible (admin-claim governance, the public security report page, feedback privacy wording, data retention, third-party data processing, upload limits, secret hygiene and production verification), and I will cooperate with School IT on verification and testing.

7. Handover and termination

  • I will keep complete technical documentation and change records, and on handover or termination I will transfer source code, documentation and access rights, rotate the relevant credentials and confirm that the school can continue to control the Website.
  • If I leave my role, graduate or cease to be responsible for the Website, I will cooperate with the school on handover and remove my own administrative and access rights.

8. Declaration

  • I confirm that this undertaking and the "Website Security, Photo Traceability & Operations Plan" are based on the code and configuration I developed. I understand that the plan is not a penetration-test or compliance certificate, and that approval must be completed after School IT verifies the live deployment.
  • I will operate the Website honestly, transparently and responsibly, and I accept the school's supervision, review and instructions.
