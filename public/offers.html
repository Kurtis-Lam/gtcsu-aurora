Welfare & Services

What We Offer

Aurora promises to provide a comprehensive range of practical welfare and quality services to make campus life more convenient and rich.

Campus Welfare Services

Campus Rentals

Umbrella & Power Bank Rental

No worry about sudden rain or low battery! The Student Union will offer free umbrella and power bank rental during regular hours.

Stationery & Printing

Emergency Stationery & Free Print Allowance

Provides emergency stationery support (correction tape, calculators, staplers) and strives for additional print quotas for every student.

Health & Care

Exam Care Packages & Health Corner

Distributing care packages during exam seasons, and setting up a first-aid kit and personal hygiene area in the Student Union room.

Partner Merchants & Discounts

Dining Discounts

Up to 15% Off Nearby Food & Drinks

Negotiated exclusive student discounts with local drink shops and restaurants—present your student ID to enjoy.

Bookstores & Supplies

Bookstore & Stationery Special Offers

Exclusive promo codes or in-store discounts for supplementary workbooks and stationery supplies.

Leisure & Recreation

Cultural & Sports Venue Discounts

Securing group discounts for local sports venues and cultural workshops.
