Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How can I submit anonymous feedback?
Fill in the title (at least 3 words) and description (at least 20 words) in the form above and click Submit Feedback.
Q: Is my submission completely anonymous?
Yes, all submissions are processed anonymously without recording any personal identification or account details.
Q: How will the Student Union handle my feedback?
Our executive team reviews submitted suggestions regularly to improve student welfare, campus events, and cabinet planning.
Q: What should I do if my submission is flagged as spam?
Ensure your submission contains constructive language, follows school regulations, and meets the required minimum word count.
Q: How can I stay updated with the latest news?
We share the latest updates on our official Instagram @gteyc_aurora2627 and on this website. Make sure to follow our IG and keep an eye on our Stories!
