As Student Union Cabinet No. 1 for G.T. (Ellen Yeung) College 2026–27, we are dedicated to bringing a vibrant and warm campus experience to all students.

Our Vision

Aurora represents the "Aurora Borealis", a natural wonder formed by the interplay of vibrant colors in the dark night sky. We chose the name Aurora because we firmly believe that every student possesses unique strengths. Just as an aurora encompasses a spectrum of diverse colors, every student on campus shines with their own distinct radiance. We hope the Student Union will serve as a bridge of communication between students and the school, working together to foster a positive campus atmosphere.

Furthermore, Aurora symbolizes "Hope and Dawn". This reflects our cabinet's sincere determination to inspire students to actively and optimistically enjoy their campus life. We will earnestly listen to students' opinions and dedicate ourselves wholeheartedly to the school, teachers, and students—striving to create a joyful, fulfilling campus experience and leaving behind unforgettable memories of youth.
