Before you download

Photos from the Aurora gallery are protected so that every download can be traced. To continue you must agree that:

  • ✓Your download digital footprint will be audited: your verified school account, the photo, the date and time, and limited device and network information are recorded.
  • ✓Each file carries an invisible digital watermark linked to your download record. It cannot be seen, but it can identify the download if the image is shared or edited.
  • ✓Records are kept for up to 365 days and are accessible only to authorised administrators and the school for investigating misuse.
  • ✓Photos are for personal, school-related use. Edits that harass, mock or misrepresent others breach the school's code of conduct.
